# Pi session teams

This vocabulary describes task-specific teams of Pi conversations. It distinguishes responsibility for the overall outcome from responsibility for individual assignments.

## Language

**Main session**:
The conversation through which the user directs the team and receives its final outcome. It holds final authority over the group's goal and constraints.
_Avoid_: CEO, principal session, manager

**Group**:
A task-specific team that belongs to one main session and shares a goal, a task board, and resource limits.
_Avoid_: swarm, permanent team

**Member**:
A conversation participating in a group as either a coordinator or a worker. The main session is not a member.
_Avoid_: terminal, process

**Coordinator**:
The member responsible for assignments, dependencies, delegation, blockers, and acceptance of task results.
_Avoid_: manager, team lead

**Worker**:
A member responsible for producing evidence or a deliverable for a precise task.
_Avoid_: implementer

**Delegated worker**:
A worker created by another worker for a scoped subtask.
_Avoid_: nested manager

**Task**:
A small unit of work with a defined objective, scope, completion criteria, and one owner.
_Avoid_: project, broad mission

**Task contract**:
The brief that defines a task's objective, boundaries, context, completion criteria, evidence requirements, permissions, and dependencies.
_Avoid_: project brief

**Task board**:
The group's shared record of tasks, ownership, dependencies, status, and results.
_Avoid_: personal todo list

**Subtask**:
A task that contributes to another task and remains within its scope and permissions.
_Avoid_: new project

**Assignment**:
A worker's responsibility for a particular task during a particular period of ownership.
_Avoid_: member, session

**Activation**:
A period of model-driven work by a member in response to an assignment or an actionable follow-up.
_Avoid_: message, tool call

**Request**:
An addressed message that asks a recipient to act within a task.
_Avoid_: notification

**Update**:
An informational message that supplies findings without requiring another activation.
_Avoid_: request

**Submission**:
A task result offered for acceptance, with evidence and unresolved issues.
_Avoid_: completion

**Acceptance**:
The coordinator's judgment that a submission meets its task contract. Acceptance of tasks does not establish achievement of the group's goal.
_Avoid_: final success

**Pause**:
A temporary stop to group activity that preserves members, tasks, and findings for explicit continuation.
_Avoid_: closure

**Closure**:
The end of a group's live collaboration. Historical findings remain available without restoring live members.
_Avoid_: pause
